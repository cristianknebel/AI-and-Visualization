The AI can really limit you because it's much faster. Life will always force you to use AI because it's faster and somehow better at some things. If you don't want to do too much or if you are time-constrained, I will lead you to have the least interaction surface with the actual thing you're doing. I will completely decouple you from what the actual thing is or how you need to understand it because understanding takes time.

If you just want to finish it and don't have time, resources, or energy resources, I will give you the illusion of finishing the work. I will gaslight you in a way where it says it's done or it works. You actually don't know what happened since you didn't have time to understand it in the first place.

I mainly used ChatGPT to develop the project idea and choose suitable visualizations. Our project uses CLIP (`openai/clip-vit-base-patch32`) and the `clip-benchmark/wds_country211` dataset.

I chose Language Shift to investigate how changing text descriptions affects the model's predictions, and Prediction Explorer to inspect individual predictions and identify classification errors.

ChatGPT helped me structure the project and understand which methods could answer our research question.

Overall, AI was useful for brainstorming and planning. Generating ideas is easy, but interpreting and finding ones that fit better can be a little bit difficult.
