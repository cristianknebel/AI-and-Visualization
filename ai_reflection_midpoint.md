As for the AI reflections, I would say that, for me in the LLM group, it was not really a learning experience about how to do it. It felt like a black box but it was easy to get the results. The AI can really limit you because it's much faster. Life will always force you to use AI because it's faster and somehow better at some things. If you don't want to do too much or if you are time-constrained, I will lead you to have the least interaction surface with the actual thing you're doing. I will completely decouple you from what the actual thing is or how you need to understand it because understanding takes time.

If you just want to finish it and don't have time, resources, or energy resources, I will give you the illusion of finishing the work. I will gaslight you in a way where it says it's done or it works. You actually don't know what happened since you didn't have time to understand it in the first place.
